[IPL STATS KNOWLEDGE BASE]
Category: Most IPL Titles by Franchise
Chennai Super Kings (CSK) has won 5 IPL titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023) under the captaincy of MS Dhoni.
Mumbai Indians (MI) has won 5 IPL titles (2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020) under Rohit Sharma.
Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) has won 3 IPL titles (2012, 2014, 2024), most recently under Shreyas Iyer's captaincy with Gautam Gambhir as mentor.
Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) won 1 title in 2016, and Deccan Chargers won in 2009.
Gujarat Titans (GT) won the IPL title in their debut season in 2022 under Hardik Pandya.
Rajasthan Royals (RR) won the inaugural IPL title in 2008 under Shane Warne.

Category: All-Time Most Runs (Orange Cap Legends)
Virat Kohli is the all-time highest run scorer in IPL history with over 8,000 runs in 252 matches, including 8 centuries and 55 half-centuries, playing exclusively for Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB).
Shikhar Dhawan is the second highest run scorer in IPL history with 6,769 runs across 222 matches with 2 centuries and 51 fifties.
Rohit Sharma ranks third with over 6,600 runs in 257 matches, with 2 centuries and 43 fifties.
David Warner is the highest scoring overseas player in IPL history with 6,565 runs in 184 matches and holds the record for most fifties (62).
Suresh Raina, known as 'Mr. IPL', scored 5,528 runs in 205 matches with 1 century and 39 fifties.

Category: All-Time Most Wickets (Purple Cap Legends)
Yuzvendra Chahal is the all-time highest wicket-taker in IPL history with 205 wickets in 160 matches with an economy of 7.66.
Piyush Chawla ranks second with 192 wickets in 192 matches with best bowling figures of 4/17.
Dwayne Bravo holds the record for former highest overseas wicket-taker with 183 wickets in 161 matches.
Bhuvneshwar Kumar is the leading Indian pacer with 181 wickets in 176 matches, winning the Purple Cap twice in 2016 and 2017.
Sunil Narine has claimed 180 wickets in 177 matches for KKR with an economy rate of 6.73.

Category: Highest Team Totals in IPL History
Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) scored the highest team total in IPL history: 287/3 against Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) on 15 April 2024 at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru.
Sunrisers Hyderabad also holds the second highest total: 277/3 against Mumbai Indians (MI) on 27 March 2024 at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Hyderabad.
Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) scored 272/7 against Delhi Capitals (DC) on 3 April 2024 in Vizag.
Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) scored 263/5 against Pune Warriors India in 2013, powered by Chris Gayle's historic knock.
Punjab Kings (PBKS) successfully chased down 262/2 against KKR at Eden Gardens in 2024, the highest successful run chase in T20 history.

Category: Individual Batting Records
Chris Gayle holds the highest individual score in IPL history: 175* off 66 balls (17 sixes, 13 fours) for RCB vs Pune Warriors India in 2013.
Brendon McCullum scored 158* off 73 balls for KKR against RCB in the very first match of IPL 2008.
Yashasvi Jaiswal scored the fastest fifty in IPL history in just 13 balls for Rajasthan Royals against KKR in 2023.
Chris Gayle holds the record for the fastest century in IPL history, reaching his hundred in only 30 balls in 2013.
Virat Kohli holds the record for the most runs in a single IPL season: 973 runs in 16 matches in IPL 2016, with 4 centuries and 7 fifties.

Category: Individual Bowling Records
Alzarri Joseph holds the best bowling figures in an IPL match: 6/12 in 3.4 overs for Mumbai Indians against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2019.
Sohail Tanvir took 6/14 for Rajasthan Royals against Chennai Super Kings in IPL 2008.
Adam Zampa took 6/19 for Rising Pune Supergiant against SRH in 2016.
Harshal Patel took 32 wickets in IPL 2021 for RCB, equaling Dwayne Bravo's record for most wickets in a single season.
